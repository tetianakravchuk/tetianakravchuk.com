## Editing and delivery

- Use a natural, calm voice at about 130 words per minute, with short pauses. Adjust pacing to fit exactly 30 seconds.
- Add narration separately so scene generation cannot alter wording or pronunciation.
- Add exact labels, metrics, and URLs in the editor. Keep captions readable on mobile.
- Export H.264 MP4 with a poster image and a caption file. Website playback should use controls and avoid autoplay with sound.
- Keep uncertainty and review labels in the finished edit. Any real product screen recording should use sanitized content.
- The website sections are ready as interactive illustrations. These prompts do not create or embed finished video files.
