How I evaluate release readiness

Evidence before confidence

Quality gates combine observable evidence, deterministic checks, safe recovery, and human decisions.

Check the source

Trace a claim to its source and keep missing or conflicting evidence explicit.

Block unsafe outcomes

Keep rights and policy constraints as hard gates; do not average away a critical failure.

Review before promotion

Require human approval for uncertain records and preserve the decision trail.

Report the actual state

Separate PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, and NOT RUN with run-specific evidence.
